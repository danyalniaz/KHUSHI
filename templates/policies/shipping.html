Shipping & Delivery Policy

Khushi Collection partners with premier express courier networks including Trax Express, TCS, and Leopard Courier to ensure prompt, secure delivery across all cities and towns in Pakistan.

1. Delivery Timeline

• Twin Cities (Islamabad & Rawalpindi): 24 to 48 Hours.

• Lahore: 24 to 48 Hours.

• Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan, Faisalabad: 2 to 4 Business Days.

• Remote / Rural Areas: 3 to 5 Business Days.

2. Shipping Charges

Standard delivery is Rs. 150 for Islamabad & Rawalpindi, Rs. 180 for Lahore, and Rs. 250 for other cities. All orders with a subtotal above Rs. 5,000 qualify for 100% Free Shipping.

3. Tracking Your Consignment

Once your parcel is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and WhatsApp update containing your courier tracking number. You can track your parcel live anytime on our website.
