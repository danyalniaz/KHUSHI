Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing, sizing, shipping, and exchanges

How do I place an order online?

Browse our collections, select your desired size and color, and click "Add to Bag". When ready, click the bag icon in the top navigation or bottom mobile bar, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or create an account for live tracking.

What are your delivery charges and shipping times?

Delivery charges are Rs. 150 for Islamabad & Rawalpindi, Rs. 180 for Lahore, and Rs. 250 for Karachi and all other cities in Pakistan. Orders over Rs. 5,000 enjoy 100% Free Express Delivery! Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), Direct Bank Transfer (Meezan Bank), EasyPaisa, and JazzCash. You can choose your preferred method at checkout.

What is your exchange and return policy?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free size exchange policy. The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in its original luxury presentation box with all designer tags intact. Simply reach out to our WhatsApp concierge to initiate your exchange.

How do I track my parcel?

You can visit our Track Order page, enter your Order ID (e.g. KC-10025) and phone number, and instantly see the real-time stage of your parcel along with the courier tracking number.

Still have questions?

Our customer support specialists are available on WhatsApp 7 days a week.
