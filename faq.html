Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about delivery, sizes, payment, and exchanges.

How long does nationwide delivery take?

Deliveries in Islamabad and Rawalpindi take 24 to 48 hours. For Lahore, Karachi, and other cities across Pakistan, express courier takes 2 to 4 business days.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) nationwide, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and direct bank transfers via Meezan Bank Limited.

What is your exchange policy?

We offer a 7-day hassle-free size exchange window on unstitched and stitched pret. If an item does not fit, WhatsApp our team at +92 300 1234567 and we will arrange a reverse pickup.

Are the perfumes authentic?

All Khushi Haute Parfumerie fragrances (like Imperial Oud) are formulated with 28% pure oil concentration using genuine aged Cambodian agarwood and last over 24 hours on clothes.
