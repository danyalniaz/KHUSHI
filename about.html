Our Heritage & Craftsmanship

The Khushi Collection Story

Where age-old Pakistani needlework traditions meet cutting-edge contemporary couture.

Founded in Lahore, Khushi Collection was born out of a profound passion for celebrating the richness of traditional Pakistani artisanship while tailoring to modern wardrobes worldwide.

Every single velvet suit, zari border, raw silk kurta, and handmade khussa in our ateliers is crafted with uncompromising attention to detail. We work directly with generational master craftsmen across Multan, Charsadda, and Lahore to guarantee authentic quality, durable pure fabrics, and ethical artisanal fair wages.
